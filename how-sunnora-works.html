Your plan, explained

How Sunnora works.

Sunnora is free Australian retirement planning software. It brings super, pensions, spending, savings and debts into one household projection, helping you explore how different assumptions could affect your retirement.

Runs on your computer · No account · No subscription

01

Start with your circumstances

Guided setup helps you enter your starting details and retirement spending. You can then add more information as you need it.

Your plan can include super accounts, other assets, mortgage and debt information, pension income and a retirement budget. For couples, linked profiles let you explore both partners’ retirement timelines within the household plan.

You can use Simple or Advanced view to choose how much detail you see.

02

Explore a projection over time

Sunnora uses the information and assumptions in your plan to calculate a retirement projection.

The dashboard brings together projected balances, income and spending. Other views let you explore individual accounts, household net worth, mortgage repayment paths and retirement budgets.

The results depend on what you enter. Different retirement dates, spending levels, investment assumptions or income details can produce different outcomes.

03

Compare changes to your plan

What-if controls help you explore questions such as:

  • What happens if I retire earlier or later?
  • How would different spending affect the projection?
  • What could a market fall mean for the plan?
  • How might extra mortgage repayments change the repayment path?

These comparisons help you understand how sensitive your plan is to different assumptions. They do not identify a guaranteed outcome or tell you which financial decision to make.

04

Understand the probability check

The probability check explores a range of simulated outcomes alongside the fixed-return projection.

It shows the share of simulations in which funds last through the modelled period, together with outcome ranges. That percentage describes the simulations under the model’s assumptions; it is not a guarantee or a personal forecast of retirement success.

05

Review pension information and assumptions

Sunnora supports modelling of superannuation, Australian Age Pension and overseas pension income, including UK State Pension entries.

Keep the information in your plan up to date, and check pension rules, eligibility and entitlements with the relevant authority. A modelled pension amount is not confirmation of an entitlement.

06

Keep your financial plan on your computer

Your financial planning data is stored locally and is not uploaded to Sunnora. The application has no advertising, analytics or telemetry.

Retirement modelling works offline. Optional features can retrieve public information such as weather, benchmarks and exchange rates. These requests do not send your financial plan.

Optional phone or tablet access is off by default. If you enable it, Sunnora must remain open on your computer, with the computer switched on and awake.

07

Know the limits

A retirement projection is an illustration based on a set of assumptions. Actual investment returns, inflation, spending, personal circumstances and government rules can differ from those assumptions.

Use Sunnora to explore possibilities and prepare questions. Check important inputs against current information from sources such as the Australian Taxation Office, Services Australia and Moneysmart.

Sunnora is a modelling and educational tool. It does not provide personal financial advice, recommend financial products or guarantee future results.
